Trust & Safety

What ZurVault Does and Doesn't Do

ZurVault is an unofficial, fan-built companion to candy.io — a browser for public Solana marketplace data on a DC Comics NFT collection. It is not affiliated with DC Comics, Warner Bros. Discovery, OpenSea, Magic Eden, or candy.io.

What it does

  • Shows public, read-only marketplace data — listings, sales, and floor prices — pulled live from OpenSea's and Magic Eden's public APIs.
  • Lets you search and filter that data by series, character, cover artist, price, and rarity.
  • Links out to OpenSea or Magic Eden when you want to buy or sell, and to candy.io when you want to read a comic you already own.

What it doesn't do

  • No accounts or logins. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to log in to.
  • No personal information collected. The only text boxes on the site are search and price filters.
  • No buying or selling here. ZurVault is browse-only. Every purchase and listing happens on the marketplace's own site (OpenSea or Magic Eden) — ZurVault just links there.
Looking for a view of the comics you already own? That's a separate product, ZurCovers, linked from the nav above.

Where things actually happen

Buying and selling happens on OpenSea or Magic Eden. Reading a comic you own happens on candy.io. ZurVault is the browsing layer in between — a faster way to search and compare across every tracked collection than checking each one individually.
