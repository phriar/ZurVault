Getting Started

How to Buy, Sell & Read

ZurVault is a front end, not a marketplace — every listing here links out to Magic Eden to buy or sell, and every comic reads on candy.io once it's in your wallet. Here's the whole path, start to finish.

1. Get a wallet

  1. Install Phantom (or another Solana wallet) as a browser extension or mobile app.
  2. Fund it with a small amount of SOL — you'll need it both to pay for a comic and to cover Solana's small network fees.

2. Buy on Magic Eden

  1. Find a comic you want on ZurVault — from a character page or the latest listings — and click through to it.
  2. That link opens the listing on Magic Eden. Connect your wallet there when prompted.
  3. Review the price, then confirm the purchase. Your wallet will ask you to approve the transaction — check the details before approving.
  4. Once the transaction confirms, the comic NFT is in your wallet.
ZurVault never asks for your seed phrase or private key, and never requests a transaction on your behalf — every purchase happens on Magic Eden's own site, with your own wallet approval.

3. Sell on Magic Eden

  1. Go to Magic Eden and connect the wallet holding the comic you want to sell.
  2. Find it in your wallet/profile view, then choose to list it for sale — you'll set either a fixed price or start an auction.
  3. Confirm the listing in your wallet. Nothing is sold yet — this just puts it up for sale.
  4. Once someone buys it, the SOL lands in your wallet automatically. You can edit the price or cancel the listing at any time before it sells.

4. Read on candy.io

  1. Go to candy.io and connect the same wallet you used to buy.
  2. candy.io reads your wallet's holdings and shows the comics you own.
  3. Open one to read it directly — no separate download or app needed.
