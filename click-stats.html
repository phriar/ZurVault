Clicks vs. Possible Sales (Weekly)

A listing click is flagged as a "possible sale" if that same listing sold on Magic Eden within a few minutes afterward — a per-listing signal, not proof any one click caused the sale (a listing clicked by several people right before it sells flags all of them). Only reflects sales tracked going forward from when this chart shipped, so early weeks will under-count until history builds up.
